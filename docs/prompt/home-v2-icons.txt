Mobile casual game home screen side buttons, six separate round icon buttons in a clean 3-column by 2-row grid, every button floating alone and centered in its cell with wide empty slate gaps between buttons. Front view, flat 2D.

Shared style for every button: a chunky glossy round candy button like a sweets-shop puzzle game. Each button is a puffy disc with a thick white rim, the disc itself filled with one saturated candy color, a big bright white gloss crescent across the upper left of the disc, a deeper shade of the same color along the bottom, and a thicker darker base lip under the button so it looks like a pressable 3D toy. The symbol on each button is big, fills about 70% of the disc, chunky and rounded, with its own white gloss highlights. One thick even dark plum ink outline closes every shape. Cel shading, cheerful and cute.
