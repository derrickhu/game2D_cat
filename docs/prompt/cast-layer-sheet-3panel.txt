One wide game-art sheet, three equal panels in a single horizontal row, each panel a tall white rectangle. Clean Japanese TV-anime cel, thin even black outlines. The attached girl is the character reference. Same girl in every panel. Pure white background. Empty image, no letters, no captions, no panel frames, no numbers.

Left panel: a full-body standing girl, straight front view, both eyes even, both cheeks even. Pearl milk-tea hair with a splash of roasted malt. Straight neat bangs. A cream barrette with milk-tea dots. On the crown a fingertip-tall bristly sea-urchin tuft. Long hair falls past both shoulders down to the waist. Oval honey-amber eyes, smug closed-lip smile, peach blush. Sleeveless orange-and-white creamsicle stripe top, white skirt printed with cartoon orange slices, tangerine-candy orange sneakers. Right hand lifts the skirt, left arm a little away from the body.

Middle panel: only her front-facing head and the front hair that sits on the face. Face, bangs, cream barrette, the sea-urchin crown tuft, and the short front locks at the cheeks. Same face as the left girl. The rest of this panel is empty white. This piece will sit in front of a dress.

Right panel: one complete long-hair hairpiece, a full milk-tea wig sprite. Long hair from the crown down to the waist, the whole back-and-side hair mass as a single piece, the same length and color as the left girl's hair. This piece will sit behind a dress. Empty white around the hairpiece.

All three panels share the same head size and the same hair color.
