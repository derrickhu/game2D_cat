A single wooden closet hanger for a mobile dress-up game, 1:1 square, on a flat cream paper field.

The hanger fills most of the canvas. A small round hook sits at the very top edge. A short neck. Then a maple-wood closet hanger the color of a new pencil: two sloped shoulders and a bottom bar with round ends, like the hangers in a kids closet.

Thin even black ink like a mapping pen. Flat cel fill. The middle is empty so the paper shows through.

Only the hanger on the cream paper. Hook at the top edge.
