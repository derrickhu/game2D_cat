# 20 对细线翅膀

画法对齐游戏里的蜜桃花瓣翼、紫藤心翼：平涂赛璐璐、中间往缝隙处变浅、细白或金色脉、外沿是同色系细笔线。一张只画一对，左右镜像，中间一条窄缝。背景整张铺石板灰蓝 #4A5A6B。

共同开头：

A costume wing pair for a cute dress-up phone game, flat Japanese anime paper-doll sticker. Straight-on back view, perfect left-right mirror. Two wings nearly touch across a narrow vertical gap in the exact center. Flat cel fills, a soft lighter glow toward the center gap, thin vein lines. Outer edge is a hairline in a slightly deeper shade of the wing color, as fine as a pen stroke. Simple graphic shapes. The pair fills the square with a small margin. Background is one flat school-slate gray-blue hex 4A5A6B filling every corner. Only this wing pair.
